# Privacy Policy

This Discord bot may process limited information required for its functionality, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, commands, and messages sent directly to the bot when necessary.

The bot does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Data is only stored when required for the bot's functionality and is retained only as long as necessary.

Server administrators or users may request deletion of stored data by contacting the bot owner.

Discord itself may collect and process information according to Discord's own Privacy Policy.

This policy may be updated as the bot changes.
